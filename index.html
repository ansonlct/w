Help

1. How to make a payment?

Enter WeChat Pay HK and open "Money" to show a payment QR and bar code. A vendor then uses a bar-code scanner or a camera to scan the QR code (or bar code) to initiate a payment transaction. If your daily transaction limit has been reached or processing an unusual transaction, you will need to enter your payment password. For optimum security, payment codes automatically update every minute.

2. Payment Amount

With WeChat’s highly secure payment system, there is no need to enter a payment password while processing less than 10 transactions a day if each transaction is less than 1000HKD. Otherwise you need to verify the payment password. The maximum daily amount of payment via payment code is 30,000HKD. To enable payment password on every transaction, go to "Payment code Management", and enable "Password Verification." The security system will intercept any suspicious transactions.

3. Payment Security

  • Each QR code / bar code is time-limited and is valid for only one transaction;
  • Be careful with your payment code and do not send your payment code to others;
  • When you first enable payment via payment code, WeChat Team issues you a certificate. If the certificate expired, you may need to re-enter your payment password;
  • If you change devices you may need to re-enter your payment password;
  • The security system will intercept any suspicious transactions;
  • Unusual activity may necessitate re-entering your payment password.

Withdrawal Notes

1. Withdrawals to local bank accounts in Hong Kong, China :

a. Users who have not completed identity verification: up to HK$500 per month.

b. Users who have set a payment password but have not completed identity verification: up to HK$3,000 per day and HK$25,000 per year.

c. Users who have completed eKYC verification: up to HK$10,000 per day and HK$200,000 per year.

d. Each user may make up to 10 withdrawals per day.

a. Existing users (Hong Kong identity card): per-transaction limit HK7,999, daily limit HK7,999, monthly limit HK50,000, and annual limit HK100,000.

b. eKYC-verified users (Hong Kong identity card): per-transaction limit HK10,000, daily limit HK10,000, monthly limit HK100,000, and annual limit HK200,000.
